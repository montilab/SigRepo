- [ ] Pre-construction validation passes.
- [ ] Post-construction validation passes.
- [ ] Clean development render passes.
- [ ] Save/upload chunks remain disabled.
- [ ] Development-only builders and candidates archived.
- [ ] Approval-status sentence added.
